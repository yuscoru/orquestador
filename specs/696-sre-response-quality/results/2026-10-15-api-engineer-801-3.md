## T013 — catalog strategy

I used controller-side reduction, not a server-side aggregation. The data layer returns every valid point in the small projection, and the controller reduces it to the latest point and a count per `(target_type, target)`.

- **Why not aggregation:** `$sort`+`$group`+`$first` cannot be exercised on a live DocumentDB from here, and plan R-4 already flags it as possibly unsupported or slow.
- **Volume bound:** about 14 targets today at roughly 1 KB per point. A year of daily evaluations is about 5,000 documents, around 5 MB per catalog request.
- **Risk:** cost grows linearly with history and is paid on every catalog request, with no cache. The checkpoint says to move to the aggregation or the `distinct` fallback if history grows by an order of magnitude, after validating it on the dev DocumentDB.
